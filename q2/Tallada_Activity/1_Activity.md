1. What is/are the parent class/es of the Programmer class in the video?

The Programmer class inherits from the Employee class, which inherits from the Person class. Therefore, its parent classes are Employee and Person.

2. If you were to add a new role called Intern, where would you place it in the inheritance hierarchy? Why?

I would place Intern as a child class of Employee because an intern is a type of employee and can inherit common attributes and
 methods, such as name and other employee information, while having its own specific responsibilities.
